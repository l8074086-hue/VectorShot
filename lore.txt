VectorPlane — Lore Notes
========================

(Working design doc. Captures the world as written so it doesn't get lost while
we keep building. Anything marked [open] is a hook we haven't nailed down yet.)

The world
---------
VectorPlane is a geometric world — another universe made of clean shapes,
angles and vectors rather than organic matter. It is not a metaphor for Earth;
it is a real place you can fall into.

The Crimson Star
----------------
The death of the Crimson Star is the inciting event. When it died it started
corrupting VectorPlane, and that corruption is still spreading.

The Falling
-----------
Humans from Earth began to fall into VectorPlane after the Crimson Star died.
A fallen human arrives as a geometric manifestation of their personality and
drive — the shape you end up as *is* who you are.

  RR      - NATIVE of VectorPlane (did not fall through). His kit is
            "Adaptation": he has no imposed human shape to be stuck in.
  TRACER  - human, fell from Earth. Fast, sharp, restless -> a TRIANGLE.
  BUD     - human, fell from Earth. Solid, stubborn, immovable -> a CIRCLE.
  NAVY    - human, fell from Earth. Disciplined, ordered, military -> a SQUARE.

(RR and Navy both render as squares in code; in-world that reads as RR being the
native baseline shape and Navy's being a human's rigid, squared-away form.)

Corruption and enemies
----------------------
The corruption of the Crimson Star is what twists the world's creatures:

  ELITES   - crystalized variants of ordinary enemies; the shard they carry is a
             sliver of the dead Crimson Star. Already implemented.
  CULTISTS - the tier ABOVE the elites. Bosses. Servants/worshippers of the
             Crimson Star's corruption. NOT YET IMPLEMENTED.
